## What changed since the first audit, and what it still shows

First audit (rooms ordered by Qloo affinity alone): 7 distinct top rooms in 15 briefs; The Blue Room topped Nashville for four of five headliners; a gallery topped Denver for one. Galleries are now excluded by Qloo category, and the same shortlist is reordered by how much more the headliner's audience likes each room than a panel of five unlike acts does (Taylor Swift, Metallica, Kendrick Lamar, Luke Combs, Billie Eilish). Qloo's affinity stays on every line; the "vs other acts" figure is shown beside it. The shortlist itself is unchanged, only its order. Result: 11 distinct top rooms in 15 briefs.

Still not good:
- **Lyric Opera of Chicago** is first for Chappell Roan. It is in Qloo's shortlist for that audience and is a large theatre; the check does not say it suits the act, and its site blocks scripts so it counts as a failure.
- **Joe's Live (Rosemont) and Infinity Park (Glendale)** fail the city test because they sit in the metro area, not in the named city. Both are real rooms; "city in address" is stricter than "in the market".
- The audit still cannot say whether a room fits a headliner's draw. That needs capacity and sales history, which Qloo does not hold.
